# Namma Yatri Ride Operations | Power BI

I built a three-page Power BI report to answer a practical question: do the zones with the most ride requests also lead on completed rides or revenue?

[View the dashboard export (PDF)](dashboard.pdf)

The report brings together ride-request funnel data, completed rides, and lookups for pickup zone, hour, and payment method. The three pages cover an operations overview, demand and revenue, and conversion and cancellation patterns.

In the coursework sample, the report reconciled 983 completed rides and ₹751,343 in completed-ride revenue. Mahadevapura led on requests, Ramanagaram on completed rides, and Bangalore South on revenue. That difference matters if you're deciding where to investigate demand, fulfilment, or average fare.

I kept quote searches and requests that received a quote separate. The same 983 completions give 67.6% against 1,455 quote searches and 77.0% against 1,277 received quotes. The funnel shows counts of recorded stage flags; it does not establish the order of individual events.

This is my Power BI dashboard from an UpGrad coursework case study. The PDF contains aggregate visuals. The source workbook and editable model are not included. The sample does not represent current Namma Yatri performance.
